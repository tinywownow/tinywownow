PDF Tools: frequently asked questions

Are these PDF tools really free?

Yes. There is no account, no watermark and no usage limit.

Are my PDFs uploaded?

No. They are processed on your device in your browser.

Can I convert a scanned PDF to Word?

Not directly. Scans contain pictures of text, so use PDF to JPG and then Image to Text (OCR) to get the text.
