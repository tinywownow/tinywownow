Crop Image

Crop Image lets you drag a selection box over your picture. Choose a free or fixed shape, such as 1:1, 4:3 or 16:9, and download the cropped result as PNG, JPG or WebP. It runs in your browser.

✓ Free forever
✓ No sign-up
✓ Files stay on your device

How to use Crop Image

  1. Add an image.
  2. Choose a shape, then drag the box and its corners to frame the part you want.
  3. Click Crop image, then download the result.

When to use Crop Image

  • You need a square profile picture.
  • You want to cut the background away from a photographed document.
  • You must trim an image to a 16:9 thumbnail or slide.

Tips for Crop Image

  • Use 1:1 for profile pictures, 16:9 for thumbnails and slides, and 4:3 or 3:4 for standard photo shapes.
  • Crop before you compress. Fewer pixels give a smaller file, which helps with passport-style photos and signatures. See the 100 KB guide.
  • The tool cuts from your full-size original, not the preview, so you keep the full resolution.
  • Save as PNG for lossless quality, or as JPG or WebP for a smaller file.

Limits of Crop Image

  • The tool offers free, 1:1, 4:3, 16:9 and 3:4 shapes.
  • The tool crops rectangular areas only, not freehand shapes.
  • The tool saves PNG, JPG or WebP.

What to do next

  • Compress ImageShrink the cropped image to a target size.
  • Resize ImageSet exact pixel dimensions.
  • Rotate and Flip ImageStraighten the picture before you crop.

Crop Image: frequently asked questions

Can I crop to a square or 16:9?

Yes. Pick 1:1, 4:3, 16:9 or 3:4, and the box keeps that ratio while you resize it.

What quality does the cropped image have?

The tool cuts from the original full-size image, not the preview, so you lose no resolution.

Which formats can I save as?

You can save as PNG, JPG or WebP.

Which formats can I crop?

You can crop JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP images.

Does Crop Image upload my files?

No. Crop Image runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device. Some tools download a helper library the first time you use them, but your files never leave your device.
