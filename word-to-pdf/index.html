Word to PDF

Word to PDF converts the text of a .docx document into a simple A4 PDF. It does not keep images, tables, fonts or styling, so use it for plain text documents rather than formatted reports.

✓ Free forever
✓ No sign-up
✓ Files stay on your device

How to use Word to PDF

  1. Add a .docx Word file.
  2. Click Convert to PDF.
  3. Download the PDF.

Tips for Word to PDF

  • It is best for plain text documents such as letters and notes. For a formatted report with images and tables, use the Save as PDF option in Word itself.
  • Old .doc files are not read. Open them in Word, save as .docx, then convert.
  • Arabic, Urdu, Chinese and other non-Latin text is replaced with question marks, so export those documents from Word instead.
  • If the PDF is too large to email afterwards, try Compress PDF.

Word to PDF: frequently asked questions

Does it keep my formatting?

No. Only the text is converted, with simple paragraph spacing. Images, tables, headers and fonts are not reproduced.

Can it convert old .doc files?

No. Only the modern .docx format is supported. Open an old file in Word and save it as .docx first.

What about non-English characters?

The PDF uses a standard Latin font, so characters outside it (for example Arabic, Urdu or Chinese) are replaced with a question mark.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Word to PDF runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device. Some tools download a helper library the first time you use them, but your files are never sent anywhere.
