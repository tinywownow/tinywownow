Rotate and Flip Image

Rotate and Flip Image turns a picture 90 degrees left or right, or 180 degrees, and optionally mirrors it horizontally or vertically. It runs in your browser and saves the result as a JPG.

✓ Free forever
✓ No sign-up
✓ Files stay on your device

How to use Rotate and Flip Image

  1. Add an image.
  2. Choose the rotation and, if you need it, a flip direction.
  3. Click Rotate and Flip Image, then download the result.

When to use Rotate and Flip Image

  • A phone photo opens sideways on your computer.
  • A selfie shows its text in reverse.
  • A scanned page arrived upside down.

Tips for Rotate and Flip Image

  • Use 90 degrees for a sideways phone photo and 180 degrees for an upside-down one.
  • Flip horizontally to un-mirror a selfie or to correct reversed text.
  • The tool saves a JPG, so transparent areas of a PNG turn white.
  • Trim the straightened picture afterwards with Crop Image.

Limits of Rotate and Flip Image

  • The tool rotates by 90 degrees left, 90 degrees right or 180 degrees only.
  • The tool re-saves the image as a high-quality JPG, so you lose a tiny amount of detail.
  • The tool fills transparency with white.

What to do next

  • Crop ImageTrim the straightened picture.
  • Resize ImageSet the final size.
  • Photo FiltersAdd a look to the finished photo.

Rotate and Flip Image: frequently asked questions

Can I flip without rotating?

Choose a flip direction and rotate by 180 or 90 degrees as needed. Combining them covers every orientation.

Does the tool lose quality?

The tool re-saves the file as a high-quality JPG, so you lose a small amount of detail.

Can I rotate by any angle?

Not yet. You can rotate by 90 degrees left, 90 degrees right or 180 degrees.

Which formats can I use?

You can use JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP images.

Does Rotate and Flip Image upload my files?

No. Rotate and Flip Image runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device. Some tools download a helper library the first time you use them, but your files never leave your device.
