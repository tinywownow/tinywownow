Extract ZIP

Extract ZIP opens a ZIP archive in your browser and lists the files inside, so you can download them one by one or all together. Nothing is uploaded.

✓ Free forever
✓ No sign-up
✓ Files stay on your device

How to use Extract ZIP

  1. Add a ZIP file.
  2. Click Extract ZIP.
  3. Download individual files, or all of them.

Extract ZIP: frequently asked questions

Can I open password-protected ZIP files?

No. Encrypted archives are not supported.

Does it keep folder structure?

Files are listed by name. Folder paths are not recreated.

Which archive types work?

ZIP only. RAR and 7z are not supported.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Extract ZIP runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device. Some tools download a helper library the first time you use them, but your files are never sent anywhere.
